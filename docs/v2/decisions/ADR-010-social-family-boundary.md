# ADR-010 - Social and Family Boundary

Date: 2026-08-02

## Context

Friend/family comparison can improve accountability but can also expose sensitive history or become coercive monitoring. It also requires a service beyond the local extension.

## Options

### A. Public leaderboards and community

- Benefit: Strong growth/engagement loop.
- Compromise: Moderation, abuse, unhealthy comparison, and privacy risk.

### B. Invite-only known-contact accountability with derived outcomes - recommended only if connected scope is accepted

- Benefit: Co-focus and accountability without raw history.
- Compromise: Identity, service, abuse, consent, and key-management work.

### C. Parent/manager domain dashboards

- Benefit: Monitoring demand.
- Compromise: Surveillance, coercion, child/employment risk, and direct conflict with Tabyss trust.

## Recommendation

Reject A and C. If connected accountability is accepted, limit it to known contacts and user-selected completion/consistency. Never share domains, exact timelines, or raw screen time by default.

Family features should be cooperative household rituals, not monitoring. Under-13 functionality requires specialist legal/privacy design and is not implied by this ADR.
