# Product Description and Positioning

## Recommended product name

Tabyss - Know Your Scroll

## Recommended category statement

Tabyss is a privacy-first productivity extension for intentional browsing. It combines accurate on-device activity tracking, focus sessions, distraction recovery, tab workspaces, and wellbeing into one browser-native product.

## One-line description

Use the web on purpose with private focus plans, distraction recovery, tab organization, and explainable insights.

## Short store description

Tabyss is a private Chrome productivity extension that helps you understand browser habits, plan focused work, reduce unplanned scrolling, organize task tabs, and protect healthy breaks. Core activity stays on your device.

## Full product description

Tabyss helps people use Chrome intentionally. It measures active browsing locally, separates focused work from unplanned drift, and turns the result into practical actions rather than another overloaded analytics dashboard.

Users can create a focus plan, open the tabs needed for a task, limit or pause distracting sites, recover when a short visit becomes a rabbit hole, and review what actually improved. Tabyss also supports eye, hydration, movement, and shutdown routines so productivity does not become overwork.

The current personality and Wrapped experience remains a distinctive reflection layer. V2 connects it to real goals: the weekly story explains not only where time went, but what the user intended, what disrupted them, what helped, and what to try next.

Core browser history and recommendations remain local. Connected integrations are optional, scoped, and disclosed before data leaves the extension.

## User promise

> Tabyss helps you plan, focus, recover, and reflect without turning your browsing history into someone else's dataset.

## Positioning options

### Option A - Private analytics tracker

Position around accurate local tracking, scores, and Wrapped.

- Advantages: Closest to the current product; smallest trust change.
- Weaknesses: Passive value; competes mainly on charts; limited behavioral outcome.

### Option B - Strict distraction blocker

Position around blocking, limits, and discipline.

- Advantages: Easy to explain; strong immediate utility.
- Weaknesses: Crowded category; easy to disable; conflicts with Tabyss's gentle voice if dominant.

### Option C - Intentional browsing system - recommended

Position around the full plan, focus, recover, reflect loop.

- Advantages: Uses current analytics and personality strengths while adding action; supports both gentle and strict users; creates a differentiated system.
- Weaknesses: Requires careful information architecture and reliable behavior modeling.

Recommendation: Option C. Tracking and blocking remain capabilities, not the entire identity.

## Message hierarchy

1. Outcome: Use Chrome on purpose.
2. Trust: Core activity stays on the device.
3. Action: Plan focus, recover from drift, and organize task tabs.
4. Evidence: See explainable daily and weekly improvement.
5. Delight: Unlock personas, milestones, and Wrapped.

## Tone

- Observant, warm, playful, and nonjudgmental.
- Direct when a rule is active.
- Never moralize a site or shame a user.
- Explain uncertainty.
- Use humor in personas and Wrapped, not in security, deletion, consent, or errors.

## Reasons to believe

- Local computation is already the product architecture, not a marketing afterthought.
- Active use, idle time, sleep gaps, audible media, site switches, rabbit holes, and media modes are handled explicitly.
- The score and persona systems are deterministic and explainable.
- Users can recategorize, ignore, export, retain, and delete their data.
